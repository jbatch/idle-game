# Enemy Identity Notes

Use these rules when adding enemy types so new introductions are readable at combat speed.

## Visual Rules

- Give every enemy a silhouette read first, color read second, and motion read third.
- Keep hostile units warmer and sharper than friendly units. Friendly units should read as cool-outlined circles; enemies should carry red/orange hostile rings or angular marks.
- New mechanics need a unique tell: ranged wind-up, support pulse, siege shock ring, stealth flicker, splitter wobble, shield face, or boss aura.
- Tutorial copy should name what the player should do, not just what the enemy is.

## Candidate Enemy Types

| Enemy | Role | Visual Hook | Player Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulwark | Shielded frontliner | Forward shield arc | Click around or bring sustained DPS. |
| Hexer | Debuffs units | Purple pulse tether | Prioritize support enemies. |
| Bomber | Reaches tower then bursts | Blinking core | Burst it before contact. |
| Splitter | Dies into smaller enemies | Cracked shell | AOE/cursor timing matters. |
| Banner Carrier | Buffs nearby enemies | Tall flag silhouette | Kill the aura source. |
| Burrower | Ignores some blockers | Dips below arena rings | Watch paths, not just front line. |
| Sniper | Long-range unit hunter | Thin aiming line | Interrupt or kill before shot fires. |

## Boss Pattern Ideas

- Retarget rule: ignore faster non-taunt units and return to tower pressure.
- Phase rule: below a health threshold, change target priority or spawn adds.
- Cooldown rule: occasional special attack with a long, obvious tell.
- Arena rule: force cursor movement with temporary danger zones.
